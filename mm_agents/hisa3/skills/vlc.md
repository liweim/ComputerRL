# Skill: VLC

- VLC cannot play DRM-protected streaming content.
- Preference changes must be explicitly saved and often require restart to fully apply.
- VLC closes when playback ends by default and has no setting to keep the finished video window open.
- VLC has no hardware sensors or ambient-light detection; video adjustments are manual.
